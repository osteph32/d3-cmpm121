# Manual test script

For a person, or an agent driving a browser. Run `deno task dev` and open the local URL with `?seed=121`. Before each section, reload. To read logical state, use `game.state` in the console. To read what has played, use the panel beside the grid, including the `#live` text, or look at the marker.

**Promotion rule:** a bug found by hand or by an agent becomes a permanent test in `core/` before it is fixed, unless it truly lives in `game/`, in which case add a step here.

| #  | Action                                                                                         | Expected observation                                                                                                                                        |
| -- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1  | Load the page                                                                                  | One canvas. A pale marker at the center of a green map. No console errors.                                                                                  |
| 2  | Tap the right arrow                                                                            | The marker slides one cell east. `#live` says `moved to 1, 0`. `player` is `{ x: 1, y: 0 }`.                                                                |
| 3  | Walk until water or rock is ahead, then press toward it                                        | The marker lurches and flashes red, then returns to its cell. `#live` says `blocked by water at ...` or `blocked by rock at ...`. `player` has not changed. |
| 4  | Tap an arrow during a walk                                                                     | The tap waits. The marker finishes the walk, then takes that step. It never skips a cell.                                                                   |
| 5  | Mash arrows quickly, then stop                                                                 | The marker settles on the cell `player` reports.                                                                                                            |
| 6  | Hold an arrow down                                                                             | The marker keeps walking. Holding into a wall repeats the bump.                                                                                             |
| 7  | Load `?seed=7`                                                                                 | A different map.                                                                                                                                            |
| 8  | On a fresh game, run `game.state` in the console                                               | The state contains `seed`, `player`, `inventory`, `changedCells`, and `won`. `won` is `false`.                                                              |
| 9  | Press an arrow several times                                                                   | Position and Terrain in the panel change as each step plays, and never ahead of the marker.                                                                 |
| 10 | Make the window short, then press the down arrow                                               | The page does not scroll. The marker moves.                                                                                                                 |
| 11 | Click the sound button, then press an arrow                                                    | The button reads `Sound: off`, and the arrow still moves the marker.                                                                                        |
| 12 | Click the sound button again                                                                   | The button reads `Sound: on`, and you hear a two-note chime.                                                                                                |
| 13 | Tab to the sound button and press Space                                                        | It toggles. Arrow keys still move the marker.                                                                                                               |
| 14 | Listen while walking and bumping (headphones or speakers)                                      | A soft tick on each step, and a low buzz on each bump. **This is the only check that a person can hear them.**                                              |
| 15 | On a high-resolution display, or at browser zoom 200%                                          | Grid edges stay sharp. The marker stays centered. Run `document.querySelector("canvas").width`: it is the canvas `clientWidth` times `devicePixelRatio`.    |
| 16 | Stand on grass or forest and press `g`                                                         | `#live` says `gathered fiber at ...` or `gathered wood at ...`. `game.state.inventory` shows the item with count 1. The cell dims. A chime plays.           |
| 17 | Press `g` again on the same cell                                                               | `#live` says `nothing to gather at ...`. `inventory` is unchanged. No chime.                                                                                |
| 18 | Walk away from a gathered cell, then walk back                                                 | The cell is still dimmed. The Terrain readout says `grass (depleted)` or `forest (depleted)`.                                                               |
| 19 | Press `g` on a cell that was never gathered, after walking back to it from elsewhere           | It gathers normally: `#live` says `gathered ...`, `inventory` increments, the cell dims.                                                                    |
| 20 | Stand on grass or forest and press `g`                                                         | The marker pulses larger then shrinks back. A two-note rising tone plays. The inventory list shows the item with count 1.                                   |
| 21 | Gather twice from different cells                                                              | `#inventory` shows both items with correct counts. The first entry does not change after the second gather.                                                 |
| 22 | Press `g` during a walk                                                                        | The gather waits. The marker finishes the walk, then pulses. The inventory appears only after the pulse starts.                                             |
| 23 | Mash arrow keys, then press `g`, then more arrows                                              | The marker always settles on the cell `player` reports. The gather animation plays in order; no event is skipped.                                           |
| 24 | Listen while gathering (headphones or speakers)                                                | A rising two-note tone on each gather, distinct from the step tick and bump buzz.                                                                           |
| 25 | Load the page.                                                                                 | The recipe list shows one button                                                                                                                            |
| 26 | Gather all the ingredients listed for cloth                                                    | Gather enough items to satisfy a recipe                                                                                                                     |
| 27 | Craft cloth recipe when inputs are met                                                         | Click a recipe button when its inputs are met                                                                                                               |
| 28 | Try to craft cloth when you are one fiber short                                                | The HUD shows that one more fiber is needed and the counter stays the sa,e                                                                                  |
| 29 | The focused recipe crafts once and the inventory shows the ingredients spent and the item made | Tab to a recipe button and press Enter                                                                                                                      |
| 30 | While the marker is moving, activate a recipe you can craft                                    | Craft while a walk is playing                                                                                                                               |
| 31 | Gather the last ingredient for a recipe                                                        | The button shows the recipe is ready only after the gather animation finishes.                                                                              |
| 32 | The marker bumps against the rock and stays on its original cell                               | Walk toward rock without a pick                                                                                                                             |
| 33 | The marker moves onto the rock and the pickaxe stays in inventory.                             | Craft a pick, then walk toward rock                                                                                                                         |
| 34 | Stone counter increases                                                                        | Stand on a rock cell with a pick and press `g`                                                                                                              |
| 35 | You enter the cell and collect one more stone without crafting another pickaxe.                | Walk onto rock with pick, then drop the pick (by spending it in a recipe, if any)                                                                           |
| 36 | With an empty inventory, try to craft the survival kit                                         | The HUD reports the missing ingredients and the banner stays hidden                                                                                         |
| 38 | Collect the ingredients shown on the survival kit recipe and craft it                          | A completetion message shows up                                                                                                                             |
